## 🚀 Flagship End-to-End Projects

### 1. 🏥 [Healthcare Operations & Readmission Risk Engine](./healthcare-operations-end-to-end-analytics)
**Tech Stack:** `SQL` `Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Scikit-Learn)` `Power BI` `Excel`
* **The Business Problem:** High 30-day patient readmission rates and billing outliers across emergency and elective departments.
* **The Solution:** 
  * Architected a 3-table relational SQL schema (`patients`, `admissions`, `billing_and_outcomes`) to analyze length-of-stay (LOS) and reimbursement bottlenecks.
  * Built a supervised classification model in Python to predict high-risk patient cohorts prior to discharge.
  * Designed an interactive Power BI dashboard tracking departmental throughput and cost-saving KPIs.

**Tech Stack:** `SQL` `Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Scikit-Learn)` `Power BI` `Excel`

### 3. 🔐 [Suspicious Login & Anomaly Detection Analytics](./security-anomaly-analytics)
**Tech Stack:** `Python` `SQL` `Machine Learning`
* **The Business Problem:** Detecting anomalous authentication patterns and potential credential compromise across enterprise logs.
